A LOWER BOUND ON KORENBLUM’S CONSTANT
CHUNJIE WANG
(Communicated by Javad Mashreghi)
Abstract. Let A2 (D) be the Bergman space over the open unit disk D in the
complex plane. In this paper we obtain a new lower bound on Korenblum’s
constant c2 : c2 ≥ 0.3554.

1. Introduction and main results
Let D be the open unit disk in the complex plane C. For p > 0 the Bergman
space Ap (D) consists of analytic functions f in D such that

where dA(z) is the Lebesgue area measure. For p ≥ 1, Korenblum’s maximum
principle (also refer to as the Bergman space maximum principle) states that there
is an absolute constant c ∈ (0, 1) (may depend on p), such that whenever |f (z)| ≤
|g(z)| (f, g ∈ Ap (D)) in the annulus c < |z| < 1, then f Ap ≤ gAp . For p ≥ 1,
let cp be the largest value of c for which Korenblum’s maximum principle holds. c2
is called Korenblum’s constant.
First conjectured by Korenblum [5], the maximum principle was proved by Hayman [3] for p = 2 in 1999. Very soon, Hinkkanen [4] proved that the maximum
principle holds for the Bergman space Ap (D) (p ≥ 1) with an absolute constant
c = 0.15724. Recently Božin and Karapetrović [1] show the failure of the maximum
principle for Ap (D) when 0 < p < 1. However, the sharp value of cp (p ≥ 1) even
when p = 2 remains unknown.
An example due to Martin (see [5]) shows c2 < √12 and, in fact, gives an upper
bound on c2 (see [7]): c2 < 0.70450 · · · . Wang [9] proved that c2 < 0.67789 · · · . On
the other hand, Hayman [3] gave the ﬁrst lower bound on c2 : c2 > 0.04. Hinkkanen
[4] proved that c2 > 0.15724. Schuster [6] showed that c2 ≥ 0.21 in term of Möbius
pseudodistance for the annulus. And Wang [10] proved that c2 ≥ 0.28185.
In this paper we obtain a new lower bound on c2 : c2 ≥ 0.3554. Our main result
is Theorem 1.
