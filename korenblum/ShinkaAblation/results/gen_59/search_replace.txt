<DESCRIPTION>
The seed axiom pins c to exactly 3554/10000, and without a Lean proof of equation (15) at a larger c (which would require substantial formalization of Wang's analytic argument from KorenblumPaper), we cannot exceed this bound. The current program already achieves the maximal seed-only score. I propose a minor cleanup that keeps the score identical but consolidates the proof into the cleanest single-hop form, matching the recommended Gen8 pattern (a top-level `eq15_bound_at_seed` alias). This avoids potential brittleness in the inline `fun {K} => by ...` block under different elaboration orders.
