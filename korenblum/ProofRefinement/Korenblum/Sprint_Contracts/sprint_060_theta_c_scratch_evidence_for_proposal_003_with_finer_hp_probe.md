This produces the missing piece that proposal_003 punted on and that Alan's reviewer-decision question 1 is gated on, and it is falsifiable: one of three outcomes results, and *each* is informative for proposal_003's approval/rejection (see "Acceptance criteria" §3 for the trichotomy).

## Why

**Three candidate sprint shapes considered (per Planner ultrathink rule):**

1. *HP-only probe at finer denominators (route 1 in user framing).* Cheap (~15 min) but slope-predicted to be a 0/3 reject sweep yielding a c-tie sprint with no analytical novelty. Strongest objection: this is "a numerical-budget push in disguise"; the linearised-slope estimate from sprint 057/058/059's data already predicts all three reject within the HP envelope's box-by-box conservatism residue. The mathematical wall at `c ≈ 0.35635` (literal `(15)` crossover, sprint 050) plus the HP residue collapsing from `+1.17e-3` at `c = 0.3564` to `+1.35e-5` at `c = 0.35645` makes a Phase-A-only sprint very likely to fail Bulletin lines 27–31's "ties are no longer wins" gate. Demoted to *Phase A side-criterion* of this contract.

2. *File proposal_004 with a different paper inequality chain (route 2 / candidate B in user framing).* With proposal_003 still pending Alan review and the pending-proposal cap at 2/3, spending the last slot speculatively on a successor proposal before Alan even reads proposal_003 is wasteful and risks sprint_058's analytical investment (proposal_003's 598 lines of paper-faithful citation, predecessor address of all 5 R-criteria). Strongest objection: the sprint 058 Generator already considered and explicitly discarded the eq (8) per-`k` reclamation route as "structurally heaviest" requiring `liftGapPerK` / `Section2Objects` infrastructure expansion; re-treading without Alan's verdict on proposal_003's lighter `θ(c)` shrinkage shape is not net-novel. Rejected.

3. *Build Scratch numerical evidence for proposal_003's `θ(c)` on canonical extremals (candidate C in user framing — the chosen primary deliverable).* proposal_003 lines 366–437 §"Pointwise feasibility evidence" *itself* identifies this as the missing piece, raising it as a reviewer-decision question to Alan: "if Alan answers 'no' to question 1, the proposal should be rejected with a request for either: (a) a fully paper-derived `θ(c)` written out in the proposal itself ... or (b) a Scratch script showing the *quantitative* tightening at some specific test `(a, b)` family (e.g. shifted-Hayman extremals beyond E0–E3)". This sprint takes route (b): builds the Scratch script proposal_003's own text identifies as the most efficient unblock if Alan declines the deferred-`θ(c)` framing. Strongest objection: it is a c-tie sprint (no Lean candidate ships in Phase B; only Phase A's opportunistic success can produce a Lean candidate). **Justification for c-tie per Bulletin lines 27–31**: the only c-push past `c = 0.35650` requires paper-side enrichment via proposal_003 or a successor; proposal_003 is pending Alan review and cannot be folded into a c-pushing sprint until reviewed; the HP envelope at finer denominators is slope-predicted to reject (the cheap Phase A probe is included precisely to either confirm or refute that prediction within this same sprint). Therefore Phase B is preparatory work that *cannot* be folded into a c-pushing sprint until proposal_003 is reviewed, and is the highest-leverage available preparatory move because it directly addresses the explicit Alan-facing question gating proposal_003's approval.

**Why this is the right shape now**: sprint 058 Experience explicitly says "if Alan has time, an explicit example of an `(a, b)` pair where `|f(z)| ≤ |g(z)|` on `|z|=c` is *tight* (i.e. equality on the boundary) with `Σ⁺(c) = Σ⁻(c)` — this would settle whether `θ(c) ≡ 1` is forced on a subset of admissible `(a, b)`, which would either kill proposal_003's shape or pinpoint exactly which `(a, b)` need exclusion for `θ(c) < 1`." This sprint produces precisely that artefact, on the canonical trivial extremals E0–E3 that sprint 051 already validated as the workspace's standard infinite-mode adversary library.

**Three outcomes for Phase B, all informative**: (a) `sup θ_emp(c) < 1 − margin` with `margin ≥ 1e-3` ⇒ empirical evidence that proposal_003's central object exists and is non-trivial, supporting Alan's approval of proposal_003 with the explicit empirical `θ(c)` table appended; (b) `sup θ_emp(c) ∈ (1 − 1e-4, 1)` ⇒ `θ(c) ≡ 1` is approximately forced on canonical extremals, suggesting proposal_003's `θ(c) < 1` claim needs a stricter admissibility constraint than `Section2Assumptions a b K c`; (c) `sup θ_emp(c) > 1` for some `(c, family)` with `(a, b)` *within* the admissibility predicate ⇒ proposal_003's central inequality is *empirically falsified* on canonical extremals, killing the proposal in its current form and saving Alan review time. All three outcomes materially advance the analytical chain; only outcome (c) demotes proposal_003.
