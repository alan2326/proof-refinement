## Mathematical insight (required, ≤ 5 sentences)

The two oracles `h_joint_phi_phip_invD2_oracle` and `h_uzz_phi_sq_oracle` produce certified upper bounds on continuous functions by partitioning the boundary `∂Ω(δ_val, B_val) × [0, p_max]` into cells and taking interval-arithmetic upper bounds per cell; cell width is `O(1/n_per_edge)`, and the per-cell looseness shrinks roughly linearly in cell width while the bound is interval-width-dominated rather than true-value-dominated. Sprint_022 measured -68.6% / -71.9% (joint / uzz_phi_sq) on the 2048→4096 doubling; sprint_024 measured -61.6% / -65.0% on 4096→8192; this sprint's planner script (`Scratch/sprint_025_planner_n16384.py`, run end-to-end with both oracles cached after run) measures the **next** doubling 8192→16384: joint drops from `2.40·10⁻⁵` (rationalised `3/125000`, ball_value `2.347·10⁻⁵`) to `1.20·10⁻⁵` (rationalised `3/250000`, ball_value `1.097·10⁻⁵`), a -50.0% reduction; uzz_phi_sq drops from `1.50·10⁻⁵` (rationalised `3/200000`, ball_value `1.419·10⁻⁵`) to `6.00·10⁻⁶` (rationalised `3/500000`, ball_value `5.78·10⁻⁶`), a -60.0% reduction. The reductions continue to be slightly sub-linear in cell width (a halving in cell width gives ~50%/60% reduction here, versus ~62%/65% in the prior doubling), confirming we are still in the cell-width-dominated regime but approaching the true-value floor — the gap between ball_value and rationalised bound is now ~9% on joint and ~4% on uzz, so future planner work on rationalisation tightening (`safety_abs`, `max_denom` retune) may extract a final ~10% before the ball_value floor itself bites. The new triangle bound `joint_n16384 + uzz_n16384/2 = 3/250000 + (1/2)·3/500000 = 3/200000 = 1.5·10⁻⁵` is dominated by `Cr_val = 1/66500 ≈ 1.5037·10⁻⁵` with a `0.25%` margin (computed in `Scratch/sprint_025_planner_n16384.py`); `norm_num` closes the rational comparison cleanly. The structural form `Cr_phi_uzz_direct_joint2 joint uzz = joint + (1/2)·uzz` (`Proof/Lib/Lemma54_Paper.lean`) is unchanged — this sprint is a pure parameter retargeting in oracle resolution, not a formula or identity change; it is the **last** sprint in this consecutive run, since sprint_026 must pivot per the same-axis ≤3 cap.
