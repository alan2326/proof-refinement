## Mathematical insight (required, ≤ 5 sentences)

Both consumed joint oracles compute certified arb-supremum balls (`ball_value`) by scanning `∂Ω(δ_val, B_val) × [0, p_max]` at `n_per_edge` cells per side; halving the cell width narrows each per-cell `acb` interval, shrinking the supremum by a factor that depends on the analytic-quotient gradient and the residual interval slack. The per-doubling reduction history on these oracles has been monotonically decreasing (2048→4096: −68.6%/−71.9%; 4096→8192: −61.6%/−65.0%; 8192→16384: −50.0%/−60.0%; 16384→32768: now measured **−41.2%/−48.2%**), confirming the bound is genuinely interval-width-dominated rather than true-value-dominated, with the per-doubling rate of decrease consistent with Lipschitz-quotient on the analytic numerator. The structural form of `Cr_phi_uzz_direct_joint2 joint uzz = joint + (1/2)·uzz` (`Proof/Lib/Lemma54_Paper.lean:567`) is unchanged — this is a pure resolution-bump sprint distinct mechanistically from sprint_026's rationalisation-tightening (which moved the rational-vs-ball gap, not the ball value); the proof body of `lemma_5_4_search` is byte-identical to V21 modulo the three numeric literals. With the new ball values `joint = 6.452·10⁻⁶` and `uzz = 2.993·10⁻⁶` and the V21 rationaliser at `safety_abs=1e-8, max_denom=10⁷`, `arb_to_rational_ub` extracts the ceilings `13/2000000` (slack 0.74%) and `31/10000000` (slack 3.57%); the resulting LHS `13/2000000 + (1/2)·31/10000000 = 161/20000000 = 8.05·10⁻⁶` is dominated by `Cr_val = 1/124000 ≈ 8.065·10⁻⁶` with comfortable integer margin `20000000 − 124000·161 = 36000`. Future sprints (sprint_028+) can revisit either further n_per_edge doubling (32768→65536, projected ~−35% reduction → ~1.4× win) or oracle-ceiling-tightening on the now-relaxed rationaliser slack (~0.74%/3.57% — the uzz oracle has 3.57% available, ~10× more than V21's residual slack).
