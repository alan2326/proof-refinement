## Goal (one sentence)

Drop V12's four η-aware oracle ceilings from `(M_phi_val=239/5000, M_phi_prime_val=543/10000, inv_F_p_z_val=2, u_zz_bound_val=2)` to their certified rational floors `(61/2500, 59/2000, 3407/2000, 15519/10000)`, and drop `Cr_val` from `1/32` to `1/300`, realizing a 9.375× reduction in the score without any structural change to the formula or trust base.

## Mathematical insight (required, ≤ 5 sentences)

V12's four numerical literals were chosen by their introducing sprints (V9's `M_phi/M_phi_prime`, V10's `inv_F_p_z`, V12's `u_zz_bound`) as integer-or-round-rational *ceilings* above the certified oracle outputs, primarily for `norm_num`/`nlinarith` ergonomics in the closing monotonicity step rather than for any analytic-content reason: the certified runner returns `M_phi_oracle = 61/2500 = 0.0244` (vs declared `239/5000 = 0.0478`, slack 1.96×), `M_phi_prime_oracle = 59/2000 = 0.0295` (vs declared `543/10000 = 0.0543`, slack 1.84×), `inv_F_p_z_oracle = 3407/2000 = 1.7035` (vs declared `2`, slack 1.17×), and `u_zz_oracle = 15519/10000 = 1.5519` (vs declared `2`, slack 1.29×). Multiplied through `Cr_phi_uzz_direct = M_phi · M_phi_prime · inv_F_p_z² + (1/2) · u_zz · M_phi²`, these per-literal slack factors compound to make the V12 literal sum `0.01267` only 12.25× *below* the literal `Cr_val = 1/32 = 0.03125` (i.e., the V12 sink wraps a `0.01267` truth in a `0.03125` cocoon). The mathematical insight is "the integer-ceiling pattern was a transient ergonomic choice in earlier sprints — the certified rationals are themselves clean enough for `norm_num` to handle the closing inequality directly, so dropping each literal to its certified value is honest and the slack vanishes." This sprint does not change the underlying formula `Cr_phi_uzz_direct`, the four oracle axioms' content, or the proof chain in `Lemma54_Paper_Pderiv_Single.lean`/`Lemma54_Paper_Main.lean` — only the literals declared in the candidate file. See [`Scratch/sprint_018_planner_tighten_literals.py`](../Scratch/sprint_018_planner_tighten_literals.py) for the exact rational arithmetic and the joint-sup analysis ruling out a structural alternative as the higher-impact move.

## Why (≤ 3 sentences, must include numerical evidence)

`Scratch/sprint_018_planner_tighten_literals.py` (verified with Python `Fraction` exact arithmetic) confirms: with the four literals set to their certified oracle values `(M_phi=61/2500, M_phi_prime=59/2000, inv_F_p_z=3407/2000, u_zz=15519/10000)`, the formula evaluates exactly to `Cr_phi_uzz_direct = 51015320591/20000000000000 ≈ 0.0025507660`, comfortably below `1/300 = 0.00333333…` (margin `46954038227/60000000000000 ≈ 0.000783`, equivalent to `23.48%` of `1/300`). The same script tested a structural alternative — minting a single new direct oracle on the analytic quantity `‖Φ · Φ' / D²‖` to replace term1 of `Cr_phi_uzz_direct` — and concluded the joint-sup analysis at the rectangle's extremal corner gives at most `≈ 0.00174`, only `~1.5×` smaller than the new literal-tightened term1 of `≈ 0.00209` — so the structural-alternative win is dwarfed by the ~5× win from this sprint's literal-tightening alone. Win factor over V12: `(1/32) / (1/300) = 9.375×`; cumulative win over V0 (`Cr_val ≈ 8.196·10¹⁴`): now `~2.46·10¹⁷×`.
