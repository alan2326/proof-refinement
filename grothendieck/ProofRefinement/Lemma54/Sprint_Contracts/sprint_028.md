## Mathematical insight (required, ≤ 5 sentences)

Both consumed joint oracles compute certified arb-supremum balls (`ball_value`) by scanning `∂Ω(δ_val, B_val) × [0, p_max]` at `n_per_edge` cells per side; halving the cell width narrows each per-cell `acb` interval, shrinking the supremum by a factor that depends on the analytic-quotient gradient and the residual interval slack. The per-doubling reduction history on these oracles has been monotonically decreasing — 2048→4096: −68.6%/−71.9% (sprint_022); 4096→8192: −61.6%/−65.0% (sprint_024); 8192→16384: −50.0%/−60.0% (sprint_025); 16384→32768: −41.2%/−48.2% (sprint_027); now measured **−28.4%/−20.9%** (this sprint) — confirming the bound is genuinely interval-width-dominated (Lipschitz-quotient on the analytic numerator) rather than true-value-dominated, with the trend now showing accelerated diminishing returns. The structural form `Cr_phi_uzz_direct_joint2 joint uzz = joint + (1/2)·uzz` (`Proof/Lib/Lemma54_Paper.lean:567`) is unchanged — this is a pure resolution-bump sprint distinct mechanistically from sprint_026's rationalisation-tightening (which moved the rational-vs-ball gap, not the ball value); the proof body of `lemma_5_4_search` is byte-identical to V22 modulo the three numeric literals. With the new ball values `joint = 4.622·10⁻⁶` and `uzz = 2.368·10⁻⁶` and the V21 rationaliser at `safety_abs=1e-8, max_denom=10⁷`, `arb_to_rational_ub` extracts the ceilings `47/10000000` (slack 1.70%) and `3/1250000` (slack 1.33%); the resulting LHS `47/10000000 + (1/2)·(3/1250000) = 47/10000000 + 12/10000000 = 59/10000000 = 5.90·10⁻⁶` is dominated by `Cr_val = 1/169000 ≈ 5.917·10⁻⁶` with comfortable integer margin `10000000 − 169000·59 = 29000` (rational margin `29000/(169000·10⁷) ≈ 1.72·10⁻⁸`). Future sprints (sprint_029 onward) face a same-axis-counter at 2 with one more resolution-doubling allowed before a forced wildcard at sprint_030, but the diminishing-returns trend (28%/21% per-doubling now) suggests the structural-pivot levers from the sprint_026 research summary (3-way decomposition, η-aware reformulation, or a non-oracle proposal for the wider analyticity domain) are the high-value moves once n_per_edge=131072 is exhausted.
