## Mathematical insight (required, ≤ 5 sentences)

The two consumed oracles produce certified arb-supremum balls (`ball_value`) by scanning `∂Ω(δ_val, B_val) × [0, p_max]` at n_per_edge=16384 cells per side; `arb_to_rational_ub` then maps each ball to a small-denominator rational ≥ ball_value, with `safety_abs` setting the absolute-margin floor and `max_denom` the rationaliser's denominator cap. Sprint_025 used `safety_abs=1e-7, max_denom=10⁶`, leaving 9.4% slack on the joint-rational (3/250000=1.20·10⁻⁵ vs ball_value 1.097·10⁻⁵) and 3.8% slack on the uzz-rational (3/500000=6.00·10⁻⁶ vs ball_value 5.78·10⁻⁶); these slacks are pure rational-rounding artifacts unrelated to the underlying analytic content. Tightening to `safety_abs=1e-8, max_denom=10⁷` (sprint_021's lever, applied four sprints back to the safety_abs=1e-4→1e-7 step at n=2048) extracts the ceilings to 11/1000000 (slack 0.25%) and 29/5000000 (slack 0.35%), consuming ~95% of the available rationalisation slack at this n_per_edge. The structural form of `Cr_phi_uzz_direct_joint2 joint uzz = joint + (1/2)·uzz` (`Proof/Lib/Lemma54_Paper.lean:567`) is unchanged — this is a pure parameter-retuning sprint on a *different mechanistic axis* from the n_per_edge resolution work; the proof body of `lemma_5_4_search` is byte-identical to V20 modulo the three numeric literals. The win-cap on this axis at n=16384: pushing further to `safety_abs=1e-9, max_denom=10⁸` would yield only 1.083× (vs 1.082× here), so this sprint exhausts the practical sweet-spot in one shot, leaving sprint_027 free to revisit n_per_edge resolution (n=16384→32768) on a fresh same-axis-counter.

## Why (≤ 3 sentences, must include numerical evidence)

[`Scratch/sprint_026_planner_rationalisation.py`](../Scratch/sprint_026_planner_rationalisation.py) ran both oracles at the V20 cell width (n_per_edge=16384) with three progressively tighter rationaliser settings and recorded: at `safety_abs=1e-8, max_denom=10⁷`, `compute_joint_phi_phip_invD2_bound = 11/1000000 = 1.10·10⁻⁵` (ball_value 1.0973·10⁻⁵; slack 0.25%) and `compute_uzz_phi_sq_bound = 29/5000000 = 5.80·10⁻⁶` (ball_value 5.7800·10⁻⁶; slack 0.35%); at `safety_abs=1e-9, max_denom=10⁸` it adds only 0.001× extra Cr_val win at the cost of 100×-larger denominators (1099/100000000, 579/100000000), so 1e-8/10⁷ is the empirical sweet-spot. The new triangle LHS `11/1000000 + (1/2)·29/5000000 = 22/2000000 + 29/10000000 = 110/10000000 + 29/10000000 = 139/10000000 = 1.39·10⁻⁵` is well below `Cr_val = 1/71900 ≈ 1.391·10⁻⁵` with comfortable integer margin `10000000 − 71900·139 = 10000000 − 9994100 = 5900` (rational margin `5900/(71900·10⁷) ≈ 8.21·10⁻⁹`); `norm_num` closes the rational comparison cleanly (the margin is within an order of magnitude of sprint_023's chosen margin `1500/(11700·10⁶)` and three orders larger than sprint_009's margin `624/25 ≈ 25` — historically all closed by `norm_num` in seconds). Two complementary research probes were also run as wildcard hygiene and documented in [`Scratch/sprint_026_research_summary.md`](../Scratch/sprint_026_research_summary.md): merged-numerator destructive-interference (`Scratch/sprint_026_planner_merged_highres.py`, ratio 1.78 at n=8192 — direction confirmed dead) and n_p_subdiv sensitivity (`Scratch/sprint_026_planner_p_subdiv.py`, ball_value drops only 0.06% from 4 to 32 — direction dead).
